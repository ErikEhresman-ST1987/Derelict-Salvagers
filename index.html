Expedition

Explore, salvage, and return to the docking point.

How to play

Tap adjacent tiles to move, or tap a known reachable tile to travel along discovered corridors. Travel stops at new discoveries. Select an adjacent bulkhead or terminal, or stand on salvage, to see actions. Moving costs 1 oxygen. Inspecting is free. Return to the blue docking tile to extract. Your progress saves automatically.
